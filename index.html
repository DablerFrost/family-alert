Put an icon on your phone’s home screen

20 seconds. No app store, no download.
Your home screen is the first screen you see on your phone — the one with all your app icons. Adding Family FlashLine there puts a tappable icon next to your other apps, so you can open it in one tap without opening a browser first. Do this first — it’s also what makes your setup save on this phone.

iPhoneYour phone

Open this page in Safari

  1. 1 Tap Share — the box with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of Safari.
  2. 2 Scroll down. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3 Tap Add — top right corner.

Done. A Family FlashLine icon is now on your Home Screen — open it from there.

AndroidYour phone

Open this page in Chrome or Opera

  1. 1 Tap the menu ⋮ — three dots, top right corner.
  2. 2 Tap Add to Home screen — it may say Install app.
  3. 3 Tap Add (or Install) to confirm.

Done. A Family FlashLine icon is now on your Home Screen — open it from there.

Will I see alerts on my watch or in my car?

On your phone or tablet: Nothing changes about the alerts themselves. The steps at the top of this guide — “Put an icon on your phone’s home screen” — just add a one-tap icon alongside your other apps, so you don’t have to open a browser first.

On a smartwatch (Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch and similar): There is nothing to install on the watch and nothing to set up there. Family FlashLine sends ordinary text messages — so if your watch already buzzes or shows your texts, these alerts will show there too, exactly like any other text.

In the car (Apple CarPlay / Android Auto): Same idea — nothing to install in the car. If your car already shows your texts or reads them out loud when your phone is connected, these alerts will come through there too, exactly like any other text.
